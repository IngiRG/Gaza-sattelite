Site guide

Guide

This site is a visual timeline based project to document the Gaza genocide from october 7th 2023 to the present day, or as close as the data allows for. Every tab except for the reports tab is updated automatically every week.

Satellite:

The satellite tab is an interactive visual comparison of Gaza pre october 7th and after. Scroll through the months post october 7th to find a clear comparison, with low sun reflection and cloud coverage. Use the split view to slide between pre and post october 7th imagery.

Casualties:

The casualties tab is a collection of casualty and injury statistics as well as summary statistics. Scroll through the timeline to get an idea of the cumulative casualties at a given date. Press a number to open a graph window to see the development of the statistics over time

Infrastructure:

The infrastructure tab compiles statistics on different infrastructure related destruction. Use the tab exactly like the casualties tab.

Military Activity:

The military activity tab is a timeline based collection of all attacks in the region involving the israeli government on an interactive map. Select through the categories to isolate them on the map. Press a circle to see the number of recorded events in that area and the geographic precision.

Reports:

The reports tab compiles significant reports regarding the genocide in Gaza. From court decidions to human rights organizations and scholars. Scroll through the timeline to see each reports release. Select category to isolate the reports source and follow links to the source material.

Victims:

The victims tab is a visualization of the identified victims of the genocide. On the histogram age distribution of victims, select category or simply select an age on the histogram itself to isolate that specific age or gender group. Below the age distribution is a dot visualization of that group, where each dot is one victim. Below that is a list of names of the victims belonging to that group.

Access:

The access tab is a collection of scraped data from UN reports, that follows the denial or impeding of humanitarian missions into Gaza. Scroll through the timeline reporting windows and note the graph visualizing denial percentage over time.

The source material is referenced in each individual tab.

Source: United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA), occupied Palestinian territory. Only periods with a comparable numerical breakdown of movements coordinated with Israeli authorities are included. Cancelled/withdrawn is kept separate from denial because OCHA attributes those cancellations to the requesting organizations for operational, logistical or security reasons. Coverage limitation: OCHA's format changes over time. Later reports without a comparable outcome breakdown are not added. The updater never fills gaps.

Recorded conflict events involving Israeli forces

UCDP georeferenced conflict events from 7 Oct 2023 onward involving Israeli forces. The map includes events inside Israel as well as Gaza, the West Bank, Lebanon, Syria and elsewhere. Where UCDP text supports a direction, events are labelled as an Israeli-attributed attack or an attack on Israel / Israeli forces; otherwise they are shown as clash / direction unclear.
Important: UCDP does not provide a universal event-level attacker field. No Israeli-involved event is discarded simply because direction is unclear. Direction labels are conservative interpretations of UCDP text metadata; ambiguous events remain visible as “clash / direction unclear”.
Context note: “Direction unclear” refers only to uncertainty about who initiated the specific recorded event. It should not be understood as describing the broader situation as neutral. Where such an event occurs in the occupied Palestinian territory, or inside the internationally recognized borders of another country, it may take place in the context of an illegal Israeli military presence. This broader legal context is distinct from the map’s event-level attribution.
